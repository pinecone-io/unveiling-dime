1. **Choose Two Large Primes**:
   - Let \( p \) and \( q \) be two large prime numbers.

2. **Compute \( n \)**:
   - \( n = p \times q \)

3. **Compute \( \phi(n) \)**:
   - \( \phi(n) = (p-1) \times (q-1) \)

   - \( e \) should be chosen such that \( 1 < e < \phi(n) \) and \( \gcd(e, \phi(n)) = 1 \). Common choices for \( e \) are 65537 or 3.
