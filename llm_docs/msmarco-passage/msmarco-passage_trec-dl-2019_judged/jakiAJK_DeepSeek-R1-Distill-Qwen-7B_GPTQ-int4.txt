   - **Characteristics**: The child has difficulty controlling facial movements due to loss of coordination between the muscles involved in facial movement and speech. This can lead to slurring of speech, difficulty swallowing, and drooping of the tongue.
   - **Causes**: Often associated with CP, where the brain does not send normal signals to the facial muscles. This is a congenital condition present from birth.

### 4. **Aphasic Dysarthria**
   - **Characteristics**: The child has difficulty producing sounds, articulating words, and forming sentences. This can result in a ""stammer"" effect, difficulty swallowing, and trouble eating.
   - **Causes**: Can be caused by CP, but it can also result from other conditions such as stroke, brain injury, or neurodegenerative diseases.

1117099,what is a active margin,"An **active margin** refers to the difference between the bid-ask spread of a security and the required margin level set by a broker or exchange. It is a measure used in trading, particularly in futures and options trading, to ensure that traders have enough funds to cover potential losses during the life of a position.

### Key Points:
1. **Definition**: 
   - The **bid-ask spread** is the difference between the price at which a security is offered for purchase (bid) and the price at which it is offered for sale (ask).
   - The **required margin** is the minimum amount of funds a trader must maintain in their margin account to hold a position.

2. **Active Margin Formula**:
   - Active Margin = Bid - Required Margin

3. **Purpose**:
   - It helps traders understand the additional funds they need to hold beyond the required margin to cover the bid-ask spread.
   - It ensures that traders have sufficient funds to cover potential losses if the price moves against their position.

4. **Importance**:
   - Helps in managing risk by ensuring that traders have enough liquidity to cover potential losses.
   - Ensures that traders can hold positions without being forced to liquidate profitable ones to meet margin requirements.

### Example:
Suppose a trader opens a long position on a security with a bid price of $100 and an ask price of $101. The required margin for the position is $95. 

- **Bid-Ask Spread**: $101 - $100 = $1
- **Required Margin**: $95
- **Active Margin**: $100 (bid) - $95 (required margin) = $5

This means the trader must maintain an additional $5 in their margin account to cover the bid-ask spread. If the price moves against the trader, they must have enough funds in their active margin account to cover potential losses.

### Conclusion:
Active margin is a crucial concept for traders as it helps manage risk and ensures that traders can hold positions without being forced to liquidate profitable ones."
