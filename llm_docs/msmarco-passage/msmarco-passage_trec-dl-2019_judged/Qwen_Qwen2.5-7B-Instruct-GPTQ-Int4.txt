### Key Components of RSA:

1. **Public Key**: This is a pair of numbers that can be freely shared with others. It is used for encrypting messages or verifying digital signatures.

2. **Private Key**: This is a secret number that is kept private. It is used for decrypting messages or creating digital signatures.

### Key Generation Process:

1. **Choose Two Large Prime Numbers**: Select two large prime numbers, \( p \) and \( q \).

2. **Compute \( n \)**: Calculate \( n = p \times q \). \( n \) is part of both the public and private keys.

3. **Compute \( \phi(n) \)**: Calculate \( \phi(n) = (p-1) \times (q-1) \), where \( \phi \) is Euler's totient function.

4. **Choose an Exponent \( e \)**: Select an integer \( e \) such that \( 1 < e < \phi(n) \) and \( e \) is coprime to \( \phi(n) \). This \( e \) is the public exponent.

5. **Compute the Private Exponent \( d \)**: Find an integer \( d \) such that \( d \times e \equiv 1 \mod \phi(n) \). This \( d \) is the private exponent.

### Public Key and Private Key:

- **Public Key**: \( (n, e) \)
- **Private Key**: \( (n, d) \)

### Encryption and Decryption:

- **Encryption**: To encrypt a message \( m \) (where \( m < n \)), use the public key \( (n, e) \) and compute the ciphertext \( c \) as \( c = m^e \mod n \).

- **Decryption**: To decrypt the ciphertext \( c \), use the private key \( (n, d) \) and compute the original message \( m \) as \( m = c^d \mod n \).

### Digital Signatures:

- **Signing**: To create a digital signature, the sender uses their private key \( (n, d) \) to sign a message \( m \) by computing \( s = m^d \mod n \).

- **Verification**: To verify the signature, the receiver uses the sender's public key \( (n, e) \) to check if \( m = s^e \mod n \).
